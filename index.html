Why this exists

The premise is simple.

Hugo likes photography. Hugo is also, unfortunately for him, very photographable. This site turns that fact into a birthday present: a clean little gallery that can start funny and become more sincere over time.

What works here

Candid photos, faux-serious captions, inside jokes, and one or two genuinely great portraits.

What to avoid

Uploading everything. The joke lands better when the curation looks intentional.

How to use it

Replace the placeholders with your own image files and keep the captions playful.

Birthday Note

To Hugo, with editorial seriousness and very mixed motives.

Happy birthday. You have spent enough time behind cameras to earn a brief, undeniable turn in front of them. This site is a record of your excellent taste, your suspiciously photogenic timing, and the fact that your friends are absolutely willing to build a whole website around the funniest available evidence.

May the next year contain good light, better adventures, and at least a few images in which you are not the one doing the framing.
